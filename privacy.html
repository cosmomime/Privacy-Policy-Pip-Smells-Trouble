Pip Spells Trouble: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Pip Spells Trouble is a word game made by COSMOMIME. This page explains what the game does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not send anything about you to us or to anyone else.

Your saved game

Your progress, settings and records are saved on your own device. If Android's backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may also keep a copy of the save in your own Google Drive backup, so it comes back when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. That copy is handled by Google and belongs to you. We never see it.

Purchases

The full game is a one-time in-app purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment under Google's Privacy Policy. The game only asks Google Play whether you own the full game, so it can unlock it and restore it on a new device. We do not receive your payment details.

Sharing results

The SHARE button only copies a short text (your result as coloured squares) to your clipboard. Nothing is sent unless you paste it somewhere yourself.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
